Direction 1 · Minimal Line

Invisible chrome

The list is the app. No borders, no cards, no container — just tasks on a white canvas. The only visual signal is the zinc aging gradient applied directly to the row background.

Aging communicates through the lightest possible touch: the background subtly shifts from pure white toward zinc-400 as days pass. Nothing competes for attention.

Strengths

  • Maximum focus on task text
  • Fastest reading scan — no chrome
  • Aging most visible against white baseline
  • Very easy to implement with shadcn/ui

Considerations

  • Feels sparse on mobile with few tasks
  • Less tactile — no affordance cues

Direction 2 · Ruled Lines

Newspaper / Legal pad

Strong ruled dividers inspired by legal pads and newspaper columns. The header and input are anchored by a thick 2px border — structural, authoritative, professional.

This direction adds an optional age-in-days micro-label (e.g. "5d", "31d ↯") — a secondary signal layered atop the background colour aging.

Strengths

  • Strong visual structure — feels "official"
  • Age labels give lawyers precise information
  • Dividers aid scanning in dense lists
  • Matches law office aesthetic

Considerations

  • More visual weight — less pure minimal
  • Age labels may conflict with ambient passive system

Direction 3 · Card Stack

Modern / Tactile

Each task lives inside its own rounded card on a zinc-100 background. The card background color carries the aging signal — fresh cards are white, aged cards drift toward zinc-400.

The stale card (32+ days) uses a dashed border to stand out from the solid-border stack. The input field is also a card, maintaining visual consistency. Inspired by shadcn/ui Card component patterns.

Strengths

  • Most tactile — feels like you can pick up a task
  • Dashed stale border highly distinctive
  • Cards naturally group aging levels visually
  • Familiar to shadcn/ui users

Considerations

  • More visual complexity than minimal
  • Cards on zinc-100 — less contrast for aging

Direction 4 · Inset Panel

Contained / Premium

The entire list lives inside a single floating white panel on a zinc-200 background. The panel has a subtle drop shadow that makes it feel elevated and intentional.

Aging rows sit inside the clean white container — the contrast between the zinc-200 outer background and the aging task backgrounds inside creates a distinct framing effect.

Strengths

  • Most premium/polished aesthetic
  • Panel clearly bounds the app's scope
  • Good contrast: aged tasks stand against inner white
  • Strong on tablet/desktop

Considerations

  • Panel borders reduce usable list width on mobile
  • Extra visual depth may distract from task content

Direction 5 · Type-Forward

Editorial / Confident

Typography at 18px is the dominant element — task text is given maximum real estate. The logo scales to 22px bold. The input's underline-only style invites typing with minimal friction.

A compact "N open tasks" counter provides status at a glance. Aging still applies via zinc background rows, but the larger text scale means aging contrast is read faster.

Strengths

  • Tasks read effortlessly — great for quick scanning
  • Confident, editorial feel matches lawyer persona
  • Aging perceived fastest at larger text scale
  • Input underline feels like a signature-ready form field

Considerations

  • Fewer tasks visible at once before scrolling
  • Dense task lists require more vertical space

Direction 6 · Intensity Bar

Data-forward / Analytical

All task rows have a white background. Instead, a 4px vertical bar on the left edge of each row carries the full aging signal — from transparent (fresh) to zinc-600 (peak). The row background stays pristine.

This approach separates data signal from readability: text is always maximally readable against white, while the bar is a compact "age thermometer." Inspired by IDE gutter markers and Kanban urgency indicators.

Strengths

  • Maximum text contrast — white background always
  • Age signal is compact and glanceable
  • Works well for colorblind users (bar is structural)
  • Most "developer / analytical" feel

Considerations

  • Bar is less ambient — requires deliberate attention
  • Less evocative of "physical aging" metaphor
  • Not as dramatic as full background shift
