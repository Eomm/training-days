### What Didn't Go Well

**Bob (Scrum Master):** "Now the harder conversation. Where did we struggle?"

**Dana (QA Engineer):** "I'll say it directly: we had two WCAG contrast failures that weren't caught until Story 5.4. Staleness text at ~3.4:1 against its background. Done/strikethrough text at ~1.7:1. Both failures. Neither was caught in 5.1, 5.2, or 5.3."

**Charlie (Senior Dev):** "In fairness, Story 5.4 was _specifically_ scoped to catch those. The contrast audit was deferred by design."

**Dana (QA Engineer):** "True — but Story 5.3 added ARIA and called itself accessibility work without checking contrast. That created a false sense of completeness."

**Winston (Architect):** "The root issue is that contrast compliance was treated as a distinct, final-story concern rather than a constraint on all visual work. Every story that touches Tailwind color classes should have contrast as an acceptance criterion, not just the designated contrast story."

**Alice (Product Owner):** "I'll own some of this — the FRs for contrast (FR26, NFR13) were mapped to Story 5.4 in the epic breakdown. That was a planning choice that deferred the risk."

**Bob (Scrum Master):** _to Nearformer_ "What's your read on this? The failures were caught and fixed — but they were introduced in earlier stories. Is the story sequencing the right place to address this, or is there a process gap?"

---

### Key Insights

1. **Contrast compliance is a cross-cutting concern, not a single-story concern.** Any story that introduces new Tailwind color combinations should include a contrast check as part of its acceptance criteria, not defer it to a dedicated story.

2. **'Previous Story Intelligence' sections work.** The explicit documentation of what changed in the prior story and what test queries would break prevented regressions. This pattern should be continued.

3. **Test growth was clean.** No story caused regressions in prior tests. The +36 test increase was fully additive. This signals good story-level scoping.

4. **The `retryFetch` pattern in 5.5 is reusable.** The `fetchKey` counter + `useCallback` pattern for triggering re-fetches is composable and worth documenting as a standard hook pattern for the project.

5. **Registration nudge is MVP-correct but feature-incomplete.** The nudge renders at 5 active todos and dismisses via `sessionStorage`. The actual registration flow (Phase 2) is intentionally absent — the nudge is a placeholder pointing toward future work.
